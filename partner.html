Additional Campus Partner Resources!

Cross-disciplinary partner organization across the University of Michigan offer specialized instructional support and workshop sessions that you can incorporate into your curriculum

  • Ginsberg Center for Community Engagement
    • Focus: Prepares undergraduate and gradue students for respectful, reciprocal community-engaged learning in civic and non-profit settings
    • Note: All first-year MSI and MHI students participate in a founcational Ginsberg workship through SI 500
    • Action: Submit a Ginsberg Center Support Request (Please request at least 1 month in advance)
  • Center for Sically Engaged Engineering and Design (C-SEED)
    • Focus: Educational sessions on socially engaged design, sociotechnical thinking, human-centered approaches, and community ethics in technical applications
    • Action:Explore C-SEED Educational Modules
  • University of Michigan Library Instructional Services
    • Focus: Specialized instruction on source evaulation, special collections, literature strategies, poster presentation, data visualization, and ciatation management tools
    • Action: Request U-M Library Instruction
